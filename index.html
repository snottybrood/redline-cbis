REDLINE INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM  ·  AS OF MAY 19, 2026  ·  UNCLASSIFIED / PUBLIC RECORD

Executive Summary
The Supreme Court's April 29, 2026 decision in Louisiana v. Callais effectively ended Section 2 VRA protections, triggering the largest mid-decade redistricting wave in modern history. Nine states have redrawn or are actively redrawing congressional maps. The projected net impact is R +8 to +14 seats — potentially sufficient to determine control of the House of Representatives in November 2026.

Strategic Assessment
Republicans hold structural advantages in 6 of 9 active states. The Callais ruling created legal cover for eliminating majority-minority districts that prior court orders had required. Democrats enacted counter-maps in California (Prop 50, D+5 seats) but lost Virginia's plan to a state Supreme Court ruling on May 8. The net shift favors Republicans by 8–10 seats with 67% probability of maintaining House control through 2026, assuming all current maps hold.
